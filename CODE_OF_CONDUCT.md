# Code of Conduct

## My pledge

I want Hinomi to be a friendly place to ask questions, report problems and share ideas, whatever your background or experience level.

## What I expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent, and disagree about ideas, not about people.
- Keep feedback specific and constructive.
- Respect that this is a small project maintained in spare time, and that "no" or "not now" is a valid answer.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling or deliberately derailing a thread.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam and unrelated promotion.

## Scope

This applies to every space around the project: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other place where someone represents it.

## Enforcement

As the maintainer I may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, contact me through the contact details on my [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Shironex). I will treat reports privately and respond as soon as I can.
